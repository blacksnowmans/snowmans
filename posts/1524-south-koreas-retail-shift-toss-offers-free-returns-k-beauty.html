South Korea's Retail Shift: Toss Offers Free Returns, K-Beauty Targets TikTok Shop, and Antitrust Regulators Crack Down on Coupang

October 27, 2026

  • Toss, a major South Korean financial-services platform, is launching a zero-fee return policy for all shoppers, intensifying customer-service competition across the e-commerce sector.
  • CJ OliveYoung, South Korea's dominant health and beauty retailer, is recruiting 300 creators for its "Shutterbrity" program to push short-form video and discovery-driven commerce.
  • Goodal Global, the company behind the hit brand Beauty of Joseon, has launched a new brand called "Depti" aimed directly at the U.S. TikTok Shop market.
  • G마켓 (Gmarket), a major online marketplace, reported its third consecutive quarter of transaction growth driven by stronger cross-border exports.
  • The Korea Fair Trade Commission strongly criticized Coupang for refusing to cooperate with regulatory investigations, warning the e-commerce giant to respect legal oversight.

Platform Wars and Regulatory Heat

South Korea's retail landscape is undergoing a structural power shift, moving away from simple price wars toward battles over customer time, trust, and ecosystem control. Toss entering the shopping arena with a zero-fee return policy is a radical move designed to remove the psychological barrier of shopping failure. While consumers welcome the protection, it raises questions about whether this hyper-competition will eventually squeeze merchant commissions. Meanwhile, Gmarket's continued sales growth shows that focusing on cross-border shipping and export logistics can successfully revitalize an established platform.

On the regulatory front, tensions are rising between the government and dominant players. The Korea Fair Trade Commission publicly rebuked Coupang for blocking investigations, signaling a harder line under the current administration against market dominance abuses and algorithm manipulation. Regulators are drawing a hard line: as tech platforms scale up, they must match their market size with legal accountability and fair play.

K-Beauty's Short-Form Push and Global Ambitions

Content-driven commerce is reshaping how brands sell, particularly in beauty. CJ OliveYoung's massive creator drive treats shopping not as a search engine, but as an endless scroll of lifestyle inspiration. Consumers no longer just look for products; they buy into stories told by creators through short-form video.

This content-first strategy is also going global. Goodal Global's launch of Depti specifically for the U.S. TikTok Shop demonstrates how South Korean beauty brands are bypassing traditional overseas retail routes to master short-form social commerce directly. By pairing viral entertainment with frictionless checkout, Korean sellers are turning global social platforms into their newest growth engines.
