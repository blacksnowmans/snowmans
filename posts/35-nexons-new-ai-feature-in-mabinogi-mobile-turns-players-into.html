Nexon’s New AI Feature in Mabinogi Mobile Turns Players into Paid Beta Testers

September 24, 2026

  • What happened: Developer Devcat introduced an "AI Connector" beta feature in Mabinogi Mobile, allowing players to use natural language commands for automated crafting and gathering.
  • The catch: Players must link external AI tools (like Claude or Gemini) and cover all usage costs themselves, while taking full liability for any operational policy violations.
  • Economic squeeze: The update coincides with tightened in-game economies, soaring gear requirements ("magical resistance"), and reduced event item drops that push players toward cash shops and the "Deca" marketplace.
  • Who is affected: Existing players who are facing compounding grind requirements, broken roadmap promises, and subtle monetization traps disguised as quality-of-life upgrades.

South Korea's gaming giant Nexon is facing fierce backlash over the latest update to Mabinogi Mobile, developed by its studio Devcat. While pitched as a revolutionary quality-of-life tool that automates repetitive farming and crafting via natural language commands, the newly launched AI Connector feature forces users to pay out of pocket for third-party AI tools and shoulder all operational risks. Combined with a sliding player base, delayed roadmap promises, and a heavily gated in-game currency economy, the feature is being heavily criticized as an exploitative corporate experiment rather than a genuine player benefit.

The controversy is compounded by aggressive economic balancing in the game's second season. High-end progression heavily relies on a "magical resistance" stat tied to rare materials—some costing thousands of dollars when converted—and a marketplace currency called "Deca." Essential event tokens used for cosmetic items have also seen stealth nerfs, forcing style-focused and combat-focused players alike into restrictive cash shop funnels. Critics argue that Nexon is leveraging its player base as unpaid lab assistants to test large-scale AI automation models, prioritizing corporate data collection over fixing a crumbling foundational trust.

Tech Innovations Fall Flat Without Basic Trust

The core frustration among players is not necessarily the technology itself, but the cynical execution behind it. In an era where mobile MMORPG markets are shifting toward reducing fatigue and respecting players' time, Mabinogi Mobile appears to be moving in the exact opposite direction—using automation as a Trojan horse to introduce new monetary sinks and operational liabilities. When developers consistently miss update deadlines, break progression paths with bugs, and then tell exhausted users to pay for their own AI assistants to handle grinding, the relationship between studio and player breaks down.

Ultimately, a flashy technical showcase cannot substitute for basic accountability and respect. If Nexon views its community merely as test subjects for internal AI strategies rather than an audience to be entertained, no amount of generative wizardry will save the game from its ongoing decline. Players want sincerity and reliable communication, not another expensive chore disguised as an innovation.
