Rhee Si-min Defends President Lee Jae-myung as Backlash Mounts Over Conciliatory Social Media Message

October 29, 2026

Prominent progressive author and former cabinet minister Rhee Si-min has urged President Lee Jae-myung not to waste political capital answering every attack, just as the president faces mounting friction within his own base over an overly conciliatory social media post.

  • The advice from a progressive heavyweight: During a recent appearance on progressive internet current-affairs broadcasts, Rhee Si-min argued that President Lee Jae-myung should dismiss bad-faith political attacks rather than dignifying them with continuous explanations.
  • Backlash from core loyalists: President Lee drew sharp criticism from within the ruling Democratic Party of Korea after posting on social media that all voters—whether supporters or opponents—are sovereign citizens whose constructive criticisms should be embraced regardless of factional lines.
  • Base grievances resurface: Frustrated loyalists interpreted the post as naive, with some critics invoking Lee's past personal controversies and using harsh metaphors to complain that the administration is taking its core supporters for granted while chasing unreciprocated bipartisanship.
  • Rhee's personal candidness: Alongside political commentary, Rhee opened up about his personal life, noting he never saved any money until his 2013 retirement from politics to focus entirely on writing.

The Dilemma of Outreach for the Ruling Democratic Party

The controversy surrounding President Lee's social media remarks highlights the persistent dilemma facing the ruling Democratic Party of Korea. Following the impeachment, removal, and subsequent imprisonment of Yoon Suk-yeol, the party's hardcore base expects a swift and uncompromising reform agenda that targets systemic abuses from the previous conservative era. To these voters, broad rhetoric prioritizing unity over partisan confrontation feels like an unnecessary retreat in the face of an obstructionist conservative opposition.

This explains why Rhee's counsel resonated so strongly on progressive current-affairs outlets like Maebul Show and Kim Ou-joon’s broadcasts. By pointing out that a government traps itself when it gets bogged down reacting to every opposition grievance, Rhee offered reassurance to supporters who want bold governance rather than defensive public relations. For President Lee, the path forward requires balancing broad constitutional appeals to the entire public with the resolute execution his core coalition demands.
