Apple’s First Foldable, the "iPhone Duo," Sets a Brutal 3.29 Million Won Price Tag

September 10, 2026

  • Apple has unveiled its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, priced at a staggering 3,290,000 won (about $2,450) in South Korea—significantly higher than US and global pricing.
  • The device features an asymmetric "D" shape, an under-display camera (UDC) supplied by Samsung Display, compatibility with Apple Pencil, and a desktop-grade cooling vapor chamber.
  • Weighing 254g, it is notably heavier than competitors like Samsung's Galaxy Fold series, forcing buyers to weigh heavy engineering upgrades against a punishing price and mass.

The tech world woke up to a seismic shift when Apple finally entered the foldable market. While many expected a conservative entry, the iPhone Duo directly addresses longstanding pain points in foldable tech—offering a seamless camera-hole-free interior via an under-display camera and an inventive asymmetric design. Yet, for all its technical polish, the reality check hits hard with its physical weight and an eye-watering Korean retail price that far outpaces international markets.

What stands out is how Apple borrowed and refined concepts long wrestled with by Samsung Electronics, South Korea's tech giant and the former titan of foldables. By utilizing active electrostatic stylus support rather than a bulky electromagnetic digitizer layer, Apple managed to support stylus input without bloating thickness—though restricting compatibility to older C-type pencils feels like a classic Apple cost-cutting compromise. Paired with a heavy-duty vapor chamber to sustain high-performance tasks, the Duo is a powerhouse disguised as a luxury gadget.

Ultimately, the iPhone Duo is a masterclass in engineering wrapped in a controversial commercial package. At over 3.2 million won, it tests the limits of consumer wallet fatigue and forces Samsung to rethink its playbook. Whether users are willing to carry a 254g brick that costs as much as a used car will determine if Apple’s latest gamble becomes a revolutionary milestone or an exercise in elite tech excess.
