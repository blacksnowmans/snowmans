Google's New Gemini Spark Shifts AI from Chatbot to Autonomous Assistant

September 13, 2026

  • Google Korea has officially launched Gemini Spark, a new autonomous AI agent feature, with full Korean language support.
  • Unlike traditional Q&A chatbots, Spark operates 24/7 in the background and integrates natively with Google Workspace apps like Gmail, Docs, and Calendar to handle multi-step tasks independently.
  • While users praise the massive productivity boost, privacy advocates and security experts have raised concerns over autonomous data access and potential security risks.

The era of simply asking a chatbot questions and waiting for a reply is rapidly closing. With Google rolling out autonomous agents, artificial intelligence is shifting from a desk-bound Q&A tool into an active assistant that executes complex workflows on its own. For subscribers of Google AI Pro (priced around 29,000 won, or roughly $21 per month), the new Spark tab inside the Gemini app allows users to set automated routines that run even when browsers are closed or screens are locked.

How Gemini Spark Works and How to Use It

Spark operates on three core pillars: Tasks, Schedules, and Skills. Users can assign specific multi-step objectives—such as researching upcoming industry events, cross-referencing them with Google Calendar, and compiling a summary report in Google Docs—without manually guiding every step. Furthermore, the Schedule feature enables background automation, allowing the agent to sift through incoming emails, organize weekly meeting notes, and draft team updates every Monday morning before the workday even begins.

However, this level of deep integration brings significant trade-offs regarding privacy and control. Because Spark can autonomously read sensitive emails and interact with external data sources, security experts warn that giving AI unchecked operational freedom creates critical vulnerabilities. Leaked onboarding warnings have already highlighted risks surrounding unauthorized data sharing, reminding users that the convenience of 24/7 automation requires rigorous human oversight to prevent costly security blunders.

Navigating Costs and Finding the Right Plan

Unlocking Spark's advanced capabilities requires moving past the free tier and into Google's paid subscription structure, which spans Plus, Pro, and Ultra tiers. For most professionals and freelancers, the Pro plan offers the best balance of price and performance, granting access to advanced reasoning and massive context windows without the prohibitive monthly costs associated with enterprise-grade Ultra subscriptions. Korean users are also finding workarounds through local telecom bundling offers—such as LG Uplus partner packages—and shared family cloud storage to keep subscription overhead manageable.

Ultimately, the rise of autonomous agents like Gemini Spark redefines the boundary between human effort and machine execution. While delegating routine administrative tasks opens up unprecedented levels of productivity, maintaining a critical eye on what your AI assistant is doing behind the scenes is essential to staying in control.
