Could a Renewed Legal Probe Force Rhyu Si-min Back Into Politics?

November 06, 2026

Speculation that prosecutors may revive an investigation into Rhyu Si-min over the Sillajen scandal could paradoxically force the prominent liberal intellectual back into front-line South Korean politics.

  • What is happening: Reports and pro-government commentators are rekindling allegations surrounding Rhyu Si-min, a former minister and influential liberal author, in connection with the Sillajen biotechnology investment scandal.
  • Internal dynamics: Coordinated attacks against Rhyu by self-proclaimed "New Lee Jae-myung" YouTube channels suggest potential top-down political maneuvering within liberal media circles.
  • The key bellwether: The standing of Hong Ihk-pyo, Senior Presidential Secretary for Political Affairs in the President Lee Jae-myung administration, is seen as the primary internal buffer against a aggressive prosecution move.
  • What it changes: Rather than neutralizing Rhyu, targeted legal pressure risks igniting intense backlash among liberal voters and compelling Rhyu to step in to reform the ruling Democratic Party of Korea.

Factions and Signals in the Presidential Sphere

The resurfacing of the Sillajen case—a biotech scandal previously probed during Mr. Yoon Suk-yeol's tenure without yielding charges against Rhyu—reopens deep fault lines. Critics argue that synchronized framing by legacy media and online commentators aims to inflict political damage under the guise of judicial procedure. Within the Presidential Office, Senior Secretary Hong Ihk-pyo, a veteran with deep Democratic Party roots, acts as a crucial check on internal factional overreach. Should Hong resign or be replaced, observers view it as a definitive green light for prosecutors to issue a formal summons to Rhyu.

The Paradox of Targeted Prosecutions

If legal authorities push forward with forcing Rhyu into a public photo-line summons, the strategy is widely expected to backfire. For the liberal grassroots base, aggressive probes against symbolic figures invoke strong memories of past political targeted investigations, triggering defensive rally-around-the-flag mobilization. While Rhyu has repeatedly expressed a preference for writing and commentary over elected office, mounting pressure could strip away that choice. Escalating political attacks may ultimately serve as the catalyst that drives him to return to active leadership and reshape the Democratic Party's internal power structure.
