Live Commerce Leaves the Discount Era Behind: Inside the Shift to AI Targeting and Vertical Brands

October 25, 2026

South Korea's live commerce sector is confronting an end to the post-pandemic shopping boom, prompting a rapid pivot away from crude, price-slashing broadcasts toward algorithmic audience targeting and specialized brand storytelling.

  • The blunt-force discount model has hit its limit: Rising customer acquisition costs and cooling consumer spending mean broadcasts relying solely on loss-leader discounts are generating vanity traffic without real purchase conversion, squeezing seller margins.
  • AI adtech takes the front seat: Global machine learning advertising firm Moloco announced its "Molocon 26" conference scheduled for November 5, underscoring how predictive AI targeting before and during livestreams is replacing generic traffic pushes to drive full-funnel sales.
  • Authentic creators replace polished sets: TikTok Shop leadership emphasized the platform's role in empowering female micro-entrepreneurs and independent sellers, proving that intimate lifestyle storytelling converts better than traditional, rigid television-style home shopping.
  • Vertical categories prove their staying power: National Geographic Apparel Kids successfully wrapped an "early bird" winter campaign, showing that real-time answers on sizing and fabric detail can overcome buyer hesitation on high-ticket seasonal goods.

The Transition to Precision Adtech

The era when simply switching on a camera and announcing a sale guaranteed explosive revenue has passed. As global e-commerce growth moderates, brands are discovering that inflated viewer counts rarely translate into bottom-line profitability if margins bleed away. This reality has pushed marketing tech front and center. Platforms and merchants are turning to automated data infrastructure—such as Moloco's machine learning engines—to identify and alert high-intent shoppers the moment a broadcast begins, cutting out the waste of generic, spray-and-pray ad spending.

Why Specialized Verticals Still Win

While broad, catch-all livestream channels are struggling with viewer fatigue, niche brands with well-defined audiences continue to generate strong returns. The success of National Geographic Kids' winter outerwear pre-order broadcast highlights where live commerce holds a structural edge over static online storefronts: immediate, high-trust interaction. Parents shopping for expensive children's jackets need granular answers about weight, durability, and fit. Resolving those concerns in real time significantly reduces return rates and secures early seasonal cash flow.

The takeaway for retail operators is clear: live commerce can no longer exist as a standalone marketing novelty. Navigating the sector's maturation requires linking viral short-form video, hyper-targeted ad delivery, and genuine two-way customer communication into a single, cohesive sales engine.
