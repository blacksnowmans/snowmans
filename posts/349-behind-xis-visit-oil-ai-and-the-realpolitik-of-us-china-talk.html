Behind Xi’s Visit: Oil, AI, and the Realpolitik of US-China Talks

September 29, 2026

  • What happened: Recent high-level discussions, including Chinese President Xi Jinping’s diplomatic engagements and US statements regarding China, reveal a pragmatic shift driven by urgent economic pressures rather than ideological alignment.
  • The oil crisis: With international crude surging past $100 a barrel, the US administration under Donald Trump faces severe domestic political risks, making China’s cooperation on energy consumption and price stabilization an absolute necessity.
  • Shifting alliances: The new dynamics have left traditional US allies like Japan scrambling after unexpected diplomatic friction, while developing nations quietly bypass strict sanctions (such as purchasing restricted Russian oil) simply to survive high inflation.
  • The AI arms race: Beyond energy, the US and China have agreed to establish dialogue and governance around artificial intelligence to prevent runaway technological risks, leaving middle powers like South Korea at risk of being sidelined in rule-making.

International news often reduces great-power diplomacy to handshakes and polite communiqués, but beneath the surface lies a ruthless calculation of leverage and survival. The recent convergence between Washington and Beijing is not a sudden embrace of friendship; it is a forced marriage born of economic vulnerability. When global inflation threatens political survival, lofty principles take a back seat to raw commodities and supply chain control.

For South Korea, caught in the crossfire of this shifting geopolitical architecture, the risks are stark. While Tokyo reels from the sudden tremor in the US-Japan alliance and global bodies like the United Nations establish new AI oversight frameworks, Seoul remains bogged down in domestic political paralysis. Without a unified, strategic control tower to navigate these seismic technological and diplomatic shifts, South Korea risks becoming a rule-taker rather than a rule-maker in an unforgiving global order.
