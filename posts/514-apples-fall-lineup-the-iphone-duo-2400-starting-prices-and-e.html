Apple's Fall Lineup: The iPhone Duo, $2,400 Starting Prices, and Everything You Need to Know

September 10, 2026

  • The Headline: Apple has unveiled its long-awaited first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro series, new Apple Watches, and AirPods 5 with built-in Active Noise Cancellation.
  • What Changed: The foldable iPhone Duo arrives with a 7.6-inch crease-free display and Apple Pencil support, while the standard Pro models drop the base tier entirely and introduce variable camera apertures and official Korean-language Siri AI.
  • Who Is Affected: Korean and global consumers face record-breaking price tags, with the iPhone Duo starting at about $2,400 (3.29 million KRW), forcing tough choices for upgraders on tight budgets.
  • The Market Impact: Analysts expect Apple to quickly capture significant foldable market share, injecting intense competition into a space previously dominated by Samsung.

The Foldable iPhone Duo and Pro Series Overhaul

Apple’s latest hardware drop makes it clear that the tech giant is done playing safe in the premium market. The star of the show is the iPhone Duo, Apple's entry into the foldable arena. Featuring a rounded design, a 4:3 wide foldable form factor, and a 7.6-inch internal display running optimized software on iOS 27, it also marks the first time an iPhone supports the Apple Pencil. Powered by the 2nm A20 Pro chip, it packs serious computing muscle—though it comes with a hefty physical footprint, tipping the scales at 254g. That makes it heavier than the iPhone 18 Pro Max, and with a starting price upwards of $2,400, it is strictly for users willing to pay top dollar for mobile productivity.

Meanwhile, the standard iPhone 18 lineup has completely sidelined non-Pro models, betting everything on the Pro and Pro Max tier. This aggressive push upmarket is sweetened by meaningful hardware upgrades, including a variable aperture camera system for precise depth control and, crucially for local users, the official rollout of Korean-language Siri AI. But focusing exclusively on top-tier specs means buyers are locked into ever-higher spending floors, pushing upgrade cycles past the three-year mark for many everyday users.

Wearables: Health Sensors and Surprise Pricing

On the wearables front, the Apple Watch 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 lean heavily into advanced health monitoring, ramping up heart-rate and HRV measurement frequencies significantly while running on the new S11 chip. The Ultra 4 maintains its appeal for endurance athletes with multi-day battery life during heavy GPS use. At the same time, the surprise star of the event may be the AirPods 5. Apple has finally brought Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) down to its open-ear base model, offering 1.5 times better noise reduction and a slew of audio intelligence features starting at a surprisingly reasonable price of about $145 (199,000 KRW).

Ultimately, Apple’s autumn showcase is a masterclass in ecosystem lock-in. The hardware engineering is state-of-the-art, but the financial toll required to keep up with these upgrades is heavier than ever. Whether consumers balk at the prices or give in once pre-orders open, Apple has once again successfully set the entire tech industry's agenda.
