Anthropic Eyes Record $1.5 Trillion Valuation in November Nasdaq Debut

November 12, 2026

AI startup Anthropic is reportedly set to list on the Nasdaq on November 19, aiming for a historic $1.5 trillion valuation that could redefine the global technology market and provide a massive windfall for its early South Korean backers.

  • Record-breaking capital raise: The company plans to raise $90.4 billion at a price range of $70 to $78 per share, dwarfing previous records held by major tech firms.
  • TSMC strategic alliance: Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), the world’s largest contract chipmaker, has approved a $500 million direct investment in Anthropic to secure its position in the AI chip supply chain.
  • South Korean market surge: Shares of Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, the world's leading memory chipmakers, jumped 3% to 5% following the news, as markets anticipate a surge in demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM).
  • Real-world utility: Beyond chatbots, Anthropic’s "Claude" model is already being deployed by major U.S. power utilities to defend critical infrastructure against AI-driven cyberattacks.

A Litmus Test for the AI "Bubble"

The target valuation of $1.5 trillion (approximately 1,894 trillion KRW) is a staggering figure that places Anthropic in the same league as established "Magnificent Seven" tech giants. While skeptics frequently warn of an AI bubble, Anthropic’s financial trajectory suggests a more robust foundation than mere hype. The company has reported revenue growth nearly 150 times higher than previous periods and has secured over $10 billion in new enterprise contracts. This IPO will serve as a critical watershed moment, determining whether the industry can finally bridge the gap between technical potential and sustainable B2B profitability.

The deal also cements a powerful "triangular alliance" between Nvidia (the AI chip designer), TSMC (the manufacturer), and Anthropic (the software modeler). By securing $500 million from TSMC, Anthropic is essentially ensuring priority access to the manufacturing capacity required to build its own custom AI chips. This level of vertical integration is rare for a startup and suggests that Anthropic intends to compete directly with the infrastructure dominance of larger cloud providers.

The "Anthropic Effect" on the Korean Economy

The ripple effects of this listing are felt particularly strongly in Seoul. SK Telecom, South Korea’s largest telecommunications carrier, stands as one of the primary beneficiaries. Its early strategic investment in Anthropic is now valued at approximately $3.1 billion (4.3 trillion KRW), providing a significant boost to its balance sheet. This comes at a pivotal time for the Lee Jae-myung administration, which has prioritized the expansion of the domestic AI and semiconductor sectors as the central pillar of its economic strategy.

For the broader Korean market, the Anthropic IPO is viewed as a "savior" for the tech-heavy KOSDAQ index, which rose 5% in a single day on the news. As Anthropic pours its $90 billion in new capital into data center expansion, the demand for South Korean HBM and server-grade DRAM is expected to skyrocket. Investors are already positioning themselves for a long-term supercycle in the semiconductor supply chain, viewing the November 19 listing not just as a corporate event, but as a catalyst for the next phase of global industrial growth.

The $1.5 trillion valuation is a massive gamble on the future of infrastructure. If Anthropic can maintain its 150x growth trajectory post-listing, it will validate the current AI investment frenzy; if not, the correction could be felt across every tech-heavy index from New York to Seoul.
