Anthropic’s Volatile IPO Triggers AI Skepticism and Rattles Korean Markets

November 23, 2026

Anthropic’s turbulent Nasdaq debut saw nearly $110 billion (147 trillion won) in market capitalization erased in a single day, sending shockwaves through global tech equities and South Korean markets.

  • What happened: After surging 17% on its first day following an $81.3 billion public offering, Anthropic dropped 8.5% on day two—falling 15% over two days as Wall Street sparred over institutional allocation bias and employee share unlock policies.
  • Korean retail hit: South Korea’s overseas retail investors, known locally as "Seohak Ants," bought approximately 220 billion won ($160 million) of shares on day one—about 20% of their initial volume during SpaceX's IPO—leaving early buyers facing immediate paper losses.
  • Wall Street fallout: The Goldman Sachs Panic Index surged to 9 out of 10 as joint drops in Anthropic and Nvidia fueled widespread skepticism over AI monetization, even after lead underwriters Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley secured roughly 150 billion won ($110 million) each in fees.
  • Domestic ripple effect: South Korea's benchmark Kospi stock index faces heightened pressure after dropping below the 4,900 level, while domestic IT services firms LG CNS and Samsung SDS have emerged as potential local beneficiary plays.

Skepticism Trumps Tech Breakthroughs

The swift reversal demonstrates a fundamental shift in how global capital markets price artificial intelligence. Even headline-grabbing research claims—such as Anthropic researchers asserting that their Claude model proved the Riemann hypothesis—failed to stem the sell-off. Wall Street is increasingly looking past technical milestones and demanding proof of commercial profitability to justify multi-billion-dollar valuations.

Implications for Korean Investors and IT Services

For South Korean retail traders, the sudden plunge underscores the risks of chasing momentum in high-profile U.S. listings. Although Korean investors exhibited more caution with Anthropic than during previous mega-IPOs like SpaceX, those who bought into the first-day rally were caught off guard by the rapid drawdown.

As the Kospi prepares for its next trading session amidst persistent foreign selling, attention is turning to domestic IT integration players like LG CNS and Samsung SDS. While these enterprise services companies may find long-term business opportunities in Anthropic’s expanded ecosystem, short-term stock performance will likely remain vulnerable to global AI valuation anxiety.
