Opposition's Golf Scandal Allegation Against First Lady Unravels

October 27, 2026

South Korea’s parliamentary audit descended into chaos after an opposition lawmaker’s claim that First Lady Kim Hye-kyung received luxury golf lessons completely collapsed within hours.

  • What happened: Representative Joo Jin-woo of the main opposition People Power Party (PPP) released blurry video footage during a parliamentary audit, claiming First Lady Kim Hye-kyung took an exclusive golf lesson at Taereung Country Club in Seoul.
  • The debunking: Professional golfer Lydia Ko, who was named as the instructor in the video, immediately refuted the claim by proving she was attending a completely different official event at the time.
  • The response: The ruling Democratic Party of Korea (DPK) denounced the allegation as clickbait-style fake news and announced plans to pursue legal action against Representative Joo for defamation.
  • What it changes: The controversy has escalated into mutual legal complaints between the two major parties, derailing policy discussions during the annual parliamentary inspection season.

Unsubstantiated Attacks and the Cost of Fact-Check Failure

The clash unfolded in the National Assembly's Legislation and Judiciary Committee, where Representative Joo pressed prosecutors based on video footage where individuals could not be clearly identified. DPK lawmakers fiercely protested, accusing the opposition of wasting state resources and engaging in reckless political theater to smear the First Lady. The failure to perform basic fact-checking—such as verifying a public figure's schedule via social media—turned the opposition's attack into a major embarrassment once Lydia Ko's team issued their refutation.

DPK leadership condemned Representative Joo's actions as reminiscent of sensationalist YouTube "cyber-wreckers," arguing that parliamentary immunity should not serve as a shield for spreading false information. Lawmakers from the ruling party accused the opposition of behaving more like "stalkers" than legislators, criticizing their focus on personal attacks rather than inspecting government policy.

Mutual Legal Complaints Gridlock Parliamentary Audits

Rather than diffusing the tension, the confrontation has escalated into a legal war. The PPP maintains that Representative Joo was conducting legitimate legislative oversight and claims DPK lawmakers obstructed his duties. Meanwhile, the DPK is pursuing formal legal complaints, seeking judicial accountability for defamation and the disruption of parliamentary proceedings. Joo attempted to deflect criticism by bringing up past political controversies, but the core issue remains his reliance on unverified reports.

This episode highlights the ongoing dysfunction within South Korea's legislative branch, where audits frequently devolve into hostile personal disputes instead of policy evaluation. With the PPP struggling to counter President Lee Jae-myung’s administration on policy grounds, attempting to resurrect personal scandal frames—reminiscent of the pervasive corruption controversies seen during Mr. Yoon Suk-yeol's term—has backfired, damaging the opposition's credibility and deepening public frustration with parliamentary gridlock.
