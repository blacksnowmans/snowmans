Apple's Latest Hardware Soars, But Korean Pricing Hits a Sour Note

September 10, 2026

  • Apple unveiled its iPhone 18 Pro series, the open-ear AirPods 5, updated Apple Watches, and its first-ever foldable phone, the iPhone Duo.
  • Technological leaps include the 2nm A20 Pro chip, a crease-free foldable display with an IP68 dust-and-water rating, and physical aperture camera controls.
  • While U.S. prices remained relatively stable—with the iPhone Duo starting at $1,999—Korean consumers face steep markups, with the foldable topping out at 3.29 million won (about $2,450) and the Pro at nearly 2 million won.

Apple’s latest product showcase offered a masterclass in hardware engineering paired with its usual aggressive pricing strategy. The star of the show was undoubtedly the iPhone Duo, Apple's long-awaited entry into the foldable market. Featuring a 7.6-inch inner display with a virtually invisible crease, an under-display camera, and an IP68 dust-resistance rating, the Duo is a formidable challenge to competitors like Samsung's Galaxy foldable line. Alongside it, the iPhone 18 Pro introduced a powerful A20 Pro chip built on a 2nm process, vastly improved thermal management via an expanded vapor chamber, and a physical variable aperture lens that moves mobile photography further away from purely software-driven tricks.

Yet, the genuine marvel of the technology is constantly undercut by Apple's commercial tactics, particularly for buyers outside the U.S. Apple Intelligence continues to be marketed as an on-device revolution, but subtle references to cloud processing and iCloud subscriptions hint at an inevitable shift toward paid AI tiers. More glaring is the regional pricing disparity: despite stable or falling exchange rates, Korean consumers are routinely hit with hyper-inflated local price tags that far exceed direct U.S. conversions. When a flagship foldable costs well over 3 million won locally, the brilliance of Silicon Valley engineering starts to feel less like progress and more like an exercise in gouging the most loyal customer base.
