South Korea Weighs Unpopular US-Led Middle East Deployment Amid Growing Political Backlash

September 14, 2026

South Korea is facing intense public and political backlash as the administration considers joining a U.S.-led military deployment to the Strait of Hormuz amid the escalating conflict with Iran, with over 60% of the public strongly opposed.

  • Overwhelming Public Opposition:
    •  Recent polls show between 51% and 60% of South Koreans oppose sending troops to the Strait of Hormuz, viewing the U.S.-led military action as lacking international justification.
  • Internal Political Discord:
    •  Ruling party member Park Joo-min publicly criticized a key government appointment regarding investigative reforms, only to be met with complete silence from his colleagues.
  • Strategic Risk:
    •  Critics, including former defense experts, warn that deploying troops risks damaging South Korea's diplomatic standing and alienating Middle Eastern economic partners.
  • Demographic Divide:
    •  While the broader public rejects the move, younger demographics show higher support, which analysts attribute to a romanticized view of military conflict.

The Diplomacy Trap: Why Joining the US-Led Campaign Backfires

South Korea’s foreign policy is walking a dangerous tightrope. As Washington pressures allies to back its Middle East operations, Seoul risks abandoning its hard-earned status as a peaceful cultural powerhouse in favor of subservient diplomacy. Critics argue that the Strait of Hormuz was blockaded due to U.S. actions, meaning South Korea has no business cleaning up a crisis it didn't create — especially when traditional Western allies have already refused to participate.

Public sentiment reflects this anxiety. With polling data showing clear opposition across nearly all political demographics outside the conservative base, pushing forward with a deployment looks less like a national security decision and more like an attempt to appease Washington at the expense of domestic stability and economic ties in the Middle East.
