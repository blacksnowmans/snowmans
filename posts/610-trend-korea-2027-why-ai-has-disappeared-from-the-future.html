Trend Korea 2027: Why AI Has Disappeared from the Future

October 02, 2026

South Korea’s annual bellwether for consumer behavior, Trend Korea 2027, has officially hit the shelves. Authored by Seoul National University professor Kim Rando, the book is a must-read for business leaders and analysts tracking the Korean market. This year’s edition features a surprising shift: the term "AI," which dominated previous lists, has been stripped from the top 10 keywords. This isn't because AI has lost its importance—but because it has become the invisible air we breathe.

  • The "AI Normalization" Phase: Just as smartphones became too ubiquitous to be considered a "trend" by 2009, AI is now the default layer of Korean life and business.
  • The "Life ROI" Mindset: Consumers are applying cold, calculated cost-benefit analyses to everything from marriage and education to daily social interactions.
  • A Shift in Innovation: Companies are moving away from purely functional tech upgrades toward "Form Factor Shifts"—reimagining the physical structure of goods to solve mundane frustrations.
  • The Rise of "Taste Capital": In an era of AI-generated content, human discernment—the ability to curate and judge quality—is becoming the ultimate competitive advantage.

The Burden of Abundance

The report highlights a paradoxical "boredom of excess." Despite being the most hyper-connected, hyper-stimulated society in history, Koreans report unprecedented levels of lethargy. This isn't the boredom of having nothing to do; it is the "busy boredom" caused by a brain fried by endless dopamine hits from short-form content. Kim Rando suggests that the solution is the "aesthetics of boredom"—intentionally unplugging to allow the mind to recover its creative spark.

This exhaustion is fueling a shift toward "Life ROI." Faced with uncertain futures and shrinking disposable income, Koreans are increasingly treating life as a transaction. From delaying legal marriage registrations to optimize tax and housing benefits, to students questioning the "return on investment" for attending class, the logic of the spreadsheet has infiltrated the personal sphere. It is a defensive reaction to a high-pressure environment, though experts warn it risks hollowing out the social fabric and meaningful human connections.

Survival Strategies for the AI Era

For organizations, the report introduces "Vibe Walking"—a methodology to move past the initial productivity plateau caused by AI. Simply using ChatGPT for drafts isn't enough; companies must restructure their workflows to treat AI agents as collaborators, with humans acting as the "orchestrators" who provide the final vision.

Ultimately, the human "edge" in 2027 will be defined by Taste Capital (Anmok-Jabon). As AI lowers the barrier to entry for content creation, the ability to recognize, curate, and appreciate genuine quality becomes rare. Whether in selecting a medical procedure or evaluating art, the "eye" of the human—trained through observation and deep context—is the only asset AI cannot replicate. In the coming year, the winning strategy isn't just to produce more, but to cultivate the taste necessary to distinguish the meaningful from the machine-made.
