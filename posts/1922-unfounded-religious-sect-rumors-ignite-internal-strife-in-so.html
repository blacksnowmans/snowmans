Unfounded Religious Sect Rumors Ignite Internal Strife in South Korea’s Ruling Party

November 04, 2026

A high-profile conspiracy theory alleging secret collusion between internal party rivals and a controversial religious sect has collapsed without evidence, triggering intense internal friction within South Korea’s ruling Democratic Party of Korea.

  • Baseless allegations collapse: Ruling party figure Kim Min-seok formally apologized during a closed-door meeting after an internal investigation found zero evidence supporting allegations of a secret alliance between party members and Shincheonji, a controversial fringe religious sect.
  • Factional backlash escalates: Prominent lawmaker Jung Chung-rae, a primary target of the smear campaign, fiercely rejected the private apology and demanded a public accounting alongside formal disciplinary action.
  • Opaque resolution draws fire: The party leadership failed to release detailed data from its promised party-wide member survey, drawing accusations from rival factions of an intentional political cover-up.
  • Opposition seizes momentum: The conservative People Power Party, South Korea’s main opposition party, launched an immediate counter-offensive, demanding that law enforcement investigate senior ruling party figures over the smear campaign.

The Collapse of a Three-Month Political Smear

For three months, the ruling Democratic Party of Korea was roiled by claims of a "three-way secret alliance" between internal party factions, splitters, and Shincheonji—a controversial religious group frequently invoked in South Korean political scandal tactics. The allegations were heavily deployed during internal primary battles to brand political rivals as factional saboteurs. However, during a closed-door general meeting of lawmakers on November 3, senior party figure Kim Min-seok admitted that internal checks turned up no supporting evidence, offering a tearful apology to those falsely accused.

Far from resolving the crisis, the admission has exposed deep structural rifts. Lawmaker Jung Chung-rae, who suffered significant political damage from the unsubstantiated rumors, publicly blasted the leadership for attempting to sweep the issue under the rug with a private apology. By withholding the detailed survey findings on party membership, the leadership's bid to quietly bury the controversy has backfired, feeding suspicions that internal democracy was compromised for short-term factional hit jobs.

Leadership Crisis and Opposition Pressure

The fallout poses a serious test for the Democratic Party leadership as it attempts to maintain legislative momentum for President Lee Jae-myung’s policy agenda. Instead of focusing on pressing economic and governance priorities, the party remains bogged down in factional warfare and credibility disputes. The smear campaign has also handed an easy political weapon to the opposition People Power Party, which is now pressing for formal criminal investigations into the ruling party's conduct.

In my view, this debacle illustrates the dangerous cost of weaponizing unverified conspiracy theories in internal party struggles. Unless the ruling party leadership provides full transparency and enforces meaningful political accountability, the lingering bitterness will continue to undermine its legislative drive and erode public trust.
