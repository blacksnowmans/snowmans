Hanmi Pharmaceutical Replaces Foreign Cloud Infrastructure with Domestic AI Tech

November 03, 2026

Hanmi Pharmaceutical, one of South Korea's leading drugmakers, is migrating the AI chatbot infrastructure of its obesity management platform Epiday from foreign cloud providers to a fully homegrown hardware and software stack.

  • What happened: Hanmi signed a multi-party partnership with domestic tech firms—AI chip startup FuriosaAI, security provider Xperix, and smart device maker Bagel Labs—to build a localized AI stack for its Epiday app.
  • What it changes: The platform will replace foreign cloud services and Nvidia GPUs with FuriosaAI’s neural processing units (NPUs) and a Korean-tailored large language model, lowering server maintenance costs by over half and improving power efficiency threefold.
  • Who is affected: Epiday users gain tighter privacy protections, as sensitive health data and prescription records will be processed on domestic infrastructure rather than routed through overseas Big Tech data centers.

Breaking Free from Big Tech Cloud Dependence

Managing healthcare platforms requires handling sensitive personal data, including user body measurements, daily diet logs, and medication histories. Relying on foreign cloud providers and foreign AI models creates ongoing risks of data leaks and leaves companies vulnerable to surging cloud operating costs driven by Nvidia's hardware monopoly. Hanmi’s shift to a sovereign AI approach ensures that user health records remain secured within local infrastructure while curbing overseas API dependencies.

The strategic move also reflects a broader shift in South Korea's technology policy landscape. After local tech firms struggled through unilateral R&D budget cuts during Yun Seok-yeol's tenure, the current Lee Jae-myung administration has prioritized building sovereign AI infrastructure and fostering local semiconductor talent. Hanmi's project serves as a key commercial test case for this national push toward technological self-reliance.

Building a Homegrown Healthcare Tech Alliance

This initiative establishes an end-to-end domestic digital healthcare pipeline. Bagel Labs supplies precise body measurement hardware like smart scales, Xperix handles digital identity authentication and data encryption, FuriosaAI provides high-efficiency NPU hardware for real-time text processing, and Hanmi delivers the consumer health service. By validating local AI chips and specialized language models in an active consumer application, South Korea's tech ecosystem demonstrates a practical path away from foreign Big Tech lock-in.
