South Korean E-Commerce Giants and Beauty Firms Bet Big on AI and Global Social Commerce

November 27, 2026

South Korean e-commerce platforms and beauty companies are accelerating their international reach and AI-driven selling strategies, anchored by a massive cross-border cosmetics acquisition and new vertical marketplace launches.

  • APR acquires US TikTok Shop standout: South Korean beauty tech company APR signed a deal worth approximately $280 million (390 billion won) to acquire Dr. Melaxin, the second-ranked skincare brand on the US TikTok Shop, cementing its footprint in North America.
  • Gmarket targets global K-beauty sales: Gmarket, one of South Korea's major online marketplaces, announced it will launch a dedicated section called "Beauty Home" early next year, pairing AI product recommendations with Lazada's network to export to 120 countries.
  • Naver integrates AI from discovery to checkout: Naver, South Korea's dominant internet portal and tech giant, is deploying AI capabilities that personalize product suggestions and connect shoppers directly to payment flows without manual search.
  • Short-form video and local support scale up: Southeast Asian e-commerce platform Shopee expanded video-commerce assistance in Vietnam, while regional Korean organizations—including local credit cooperative Saemaul Geumgo—rolled out live commerce initiatives for small businesses and farmers.

AI and Vertical Platforms Shift the Retail Playbook

The latest industry moves reflect a clear shift in South Korean e-commerce strategy away from generic online storefronts toward specialized vertical hubs powered by artificial intelligence. Gmarket’s upcoming dedicated beauty channel shows that open marketplaces can no longer rely solely on keyword search algorithms. By combining AI recommendation engines with automated cross-border logistics via Southeast Asian platform Lazada, Gmarket is trying to give domestic cosmetics sellers immediate access to overseas buyers without requiring them to build separate local supply chains.

At the same time, Naver’s end-to-end AI shopping push signals that algorithmic content matching is replacing standard price-comparison models. Merchants operating on Korean platforms now face pressure to produce dynamic video content and optimized product data, as AI recommendation logic increasingly determines which products get featured. This trend is visible across all tiers of Korean retail, as even regional agricultural programs in places like Bonghwa County are now training local farmers to generate marketing copy and live-stream scripts using generative AI.

Viral Video Validates Global K-Beauty Valuations

APR’s $280 million acquisition of Dr. Melaxin demonstrates the real financial scale now backing brands built on social commerce platform feeds. By absorbing a top-performing skincare label on the US TikTok Shop, APR diversifies beyond its flagship Medi-Cube line and gains an established viral sales channel in North America. The deal sets a clear precedent for Korean indie cosmetics brands: building dedicated followings on short-form video channels can rapidly lead to multi-hundred-million-dollar corporate valuations.
